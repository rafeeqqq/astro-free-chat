AI free chat that hands users to a real astrologer

AI reads the kundli and gives a short, specific free reading. A real astrologer gives the upay once the user recharges. The pilot starts on WhatsApp with a small group of lapsed payers.

Owner: Rafeeq24 Sep 2026Pilot: WhatsApp, ~1,200 lapsed payersMetric: recharge within 7 days

The bet

We don't offer free chat today, because every free minute costs a human astrologer's time and we're already short on supply. AI takes that cost away. Sitara showed that AI doesn't sell well as the paid product, so here the AI doesn't try to. It creates curiosity and passes the user on.

If AI gives lapsed payers a free, personal reading and then hands them to a real astrologer, more of them will recharge within 7 days than a holdout that gets nothing.

What AT does today

Tested 24 Sep as a new user. The whole chat takes about 2 minutes.

  • Kundli collected before the chat. Name, gender, DOB, time and place, with "don't know my time, still ~80% accurate".
  • Scripted reading. Kundli image, then moon sign, then current dasha, then a hopeful "transformation in 2026".
  • Never answers the question. "When will I get married" gets "checking your grahas" and more vague positive lines.
  • Offers it for money twice. A "100% cashback" strip with Recharge at the top, and "continue at ₹26/min" at the bottom.
  • No AI label. Only "automated message to confirm chat has started".

Where we beat it

We keep what works and fix what's weak.

  • Keep: kundli before chat, short fast messages, the kundli image, facts before hope, the cashback strip.
  • Answer part of the question. Give one specific point from the chart, then hold back the upay for the astrologer.
  • For returning users, open on the topic of their last chat. AT can't do this.
  • Hand-off to a real astrologer who already has the context, so the user never repeats themselves.
  • Prompt editable from admin, so we can change it daily without a release.

Pilot design

Small, fast and measurable. The holdout isn't optional: our August WhatsApp win-back test did worse than its control on revenue.

Who
Lapsed payers

Recharged at least once, no booking in 14–60 days, birth details on file.

How many
~1,200 users, split 50/50

Enough to see 7-day recharge move from about 4% to 8%. Holdout gets no message.

Primary metric
Recharge within 7 days

Per targeted user, treatment vs. holdout. Not per user who replied.

Also track
Reply → hand-off → recharge

Revenue at day 14, talk time with the human astrologer, opt-outs and complaints.

Duration
2 weeks

Read 20 chats per prompt version by hand before changing it.

Stop rule
2 versions, no lift → stop

No lift at 2 weeks: change the hook. Still none: shut it down.

What would sink this

A generic hook. Our August WhatsApp win-back test did worse than doing nothing (ARPU ₹11 vs ₹85). If the first message reads like a mass blast, this repeats. The hook must come from the user's own last chat and kundli.

A broken hand-off. If the human astrologer asks for birth details again right after the user pays, the user feels tricked. The pre-filled context message is a must-have, not an extra.
